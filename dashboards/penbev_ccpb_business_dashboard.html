Units of Measure & Packaging

Bulk-selling rule — PenBev does not sell loose units

The consumer EA (each) exists only so a single bottle can be scanned at retail — it is IsSellable=1, IsOrderable=0. Trade orders are placed in cases (PK2) and pallets (PLT). Every sales/inventory fact is therefore in a bulk UoM, and EA appears only on the shelf-edge barcode.
UoMDescriptionConv → EASellableOrderableWarehouse
EAEach sellable unit (scan only)1YESnono
PK1Inner pack / shrink pack6noYESYES
PK2Outer case (primary order unit)24noYESYES
PLTPallet1,440noYESYES
Conversions vary by pack size (e.g. a 2L case = 6 EA, a 330ml case = 24 EA). Each UoM row carries gross/net weight, volume in litres and physical dimensions (H×W×D cm) for load planning.
