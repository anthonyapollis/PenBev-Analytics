Resolution flow

1 · scan stg.Product for EA codes used by >1 SKU
2 · register clash in dq.BarcodeCollision
3 · pick primary (orderable → lowest SKU)
4 · DimProduct: set BarcodeIsShared, PrimarySKUForBarcode
5 · facts join via primary → no double-count
6 · MDM re-barcodes the losers with new GS1 codes
