01
Critical Priority   Trade Investment · Mass Retail

Rebalance Trade Spend: Shift 5% from Mass Retail to Convenience & E-Commerce

Mass Retail absorbs 12-15% trade spend yet delivers lower net margin per rand than Convenience (3-5% trade spend, higher RSP). A 5% reallocation of the Mass Retail trade budget to targeted Convenience activations and Checkers Sixty60 / Pick n Pay asap! digital promotions would generate equivalent volume uplift at 40% higher gross margin — an estimated R840K net margin improvement annually at current revenue levels.

02
Critical Priority   SKU Rationalisation · Portfolio Health

Delist D-Grade SKUs — Remove 25 Under-Performing Variants

10% of SKUs (approximately 25 variants) are classified D-grade — below-hurdle margin, sub-50% weighted distribution, and negligible volume contribution. Each D-grade SKU consumes warehouse slot costs, sales force call time, and master data maintenance. Delisting the bottom quartile simplifies the range, redirects trade investment to A/B-grade performers, and typically improves total portfolio margin by 2-3 percentage points.

25 D-grade SKU candidates · 2-3pp margin improvement on rationalisation

03
High Priority   Distribution · Energy Category

Drive Monster & Powerplay Weighted Distribution from 44% to 70%

Energy drinks are the fastest-growing category (22% YoY) but are available in only 44% of weighted outlets — meaning they're absent from over half the volume. Prioritising energy drink distribution in Convenience and Petrol & Travel (highest energy drink impulse channels) would capture incremental volume without additional trade spend. At 70% Wt. Dist., energy drinks would contribute R1.2M in additional annual revenue at current off-take rates.

04
High Priority   Geography · Province Diversification

Reduce Gauteng Revenue Concentration — Target KZN & Western Cape Growth

Gauteng generates 32% of revenue at R2,580 per capita vs. national average R1,210 — a 2.1× overindex. KwaZulu-Natal (18% share) and Western Cape (22% share) are both under-indexed relative to GDP. Deploying targeted route-to-market investment in coastal provinces reduces single-province revenue risk while capturing mid-tier market growth in Durban's informal trade and Cape Town's premium convenience channel.

05
High Priority   Pricing · Cappy Premium Portfolio

Elevate Cappy Pricing in Petrol & Travel by 8% — Zero Volume Risk

Cappy (juices & nectars) has the highest margin per unit but the lowest weighted distribution (62%) relative to its margin potential. In Petrol & Travel — a captive, price-inelastic channel — an 8% RSP increase on 250ml and 300ml chilled formats would add R0.40-R0.55 margin per unit sold with negligible volume elasticity. Petrol & Travel shoppers are the least price-sensitive segment in the portfolio.

06
Medium Priority   E-Commerce · Digital Channel

Build a Dedicated E-Commerce SKU Range — 30 Digital-First Formats

E-Commerce (12% revenue, growing) is served by the same range as physical retail — but digital shoppers have different format preferences: multipack, bulk, and subscription-friendly formats outperform single-serve in online baskets. Creating a 30-SKU digital-first range (4-pack, 6-pack, and 1.5L multipack formats) would increase average basket value on Checkers Sixty60 and Pick n Pay asap! by an estimated 22%.

07
Medium Priority   Analytics · Trade ROI Model

Instrument Trade Spend with Redemption Tracking — Enable True ROI Model

The Trade Spend ROI Predictor requires actual promotional redemption data (uplift volumes by promotion mechanic) to produce a learnable signal. Connecting PenBev's promotional calendar to POS scanner data from retailers — available via Nielsen Connect or SPAR Analytics — would provide the volume uplift data needed to train a production-grade ROI model. Industry benchmarks show 15-25% trade spend efficiency gains from model-guided allocation.

08
Medium Priority   Northern Cape & Limpopo · Informal Trade

Deploy Wholesale Channel as Informal Trade Gateway in Low-Penetration Provinces

Northern Cape (3% revenue share) and Limpopo (4% share) have modern retail penetration below 35% — informal spaza shops and taverns dominate. Wholesale is the most efficient route-to-market in these provinces: distributors handle last-mile delivery at lower cost than direct sales force. A focused wholesale partnership programme in Limpopo and Northern Cape could add R620K in annual revenue at minimal incremental sales cost.

09
Medium Priority   Powerade · Sports Channel

Launch Powerade Gym & Fitness Channel Programme — 150 Outlets

Powerade has the lowest weighted distribution (44%) in the portfolio but operates in the highest-growth segment after energy drinks. A targeted gym and fitness centre channel programme — 150 outlets in Gauteng, Western Cape, and KZN — would capture the pre/post-workout consumption occasion currently served by on-trade on-premise competitors. At R18 RSP per 500ml, 150 outlets × 40 units/week = R5.6M annual incremental revenue.

Medium Priority   CCPB Dashboard · Reporting Automation

Automate Monthly CCPB Reporting — Replace Manual Excel with Live Dashboard

The current CCPB reporting cycle takes 3-4 days of analyst time per month: extracting from ERP, cleaning, building pivot tables, and distributing Excel files. Connecting the existing dashboards (penbev_ccpb_business_dashboard.html, health_dashboard.html) to a live data feed via an API layer eliminates the monthly manual cycle, reduces reporting errors, and gives the commercial team real-time access to margin, distribution, and trade ROI data — at the click of a button.
