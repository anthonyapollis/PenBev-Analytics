Business Context

PenBev is a South African FMCG beverage distributor operating under a Consumer Channel Price-by-Brand (CCPB) commercial model. CCPB pricing means each brand × channel × pack size combination carries its own negotiated price point — creating a complex SKU master that requires dedicated analytics infrastructure to manage profitability, distribution coverage, and trade investment effectively.

Technical Deliverables

  • 📊 4 HTML Dashboards — Business, Health, Product Master, Executive
  • 📁 Excel Workbook — 5-tab KPI, brand, province, ML, and product sheets
  • 🗺 Distribution Map — SA outlet network with channel filtering
  • 🤖 3 ML Models — SKU grader, revenue predictor, trade ROI model
  • 📦 Product Master — 246 SKUs with barcode, cost, margin, and grade data

Mass Retail Dominates

35% of revenue but absorbs 12-15% trade spend. Checkers, Pick n Pay, and SPAR drive the majority of carbonated soft drink volume with aggressive promotional calendars requiring proactive trade investment planning.

E-Commerce Growing Fast

12% revenue share and accelerating. Checkers Sixty60, Pick n Pay asap!, and Woolworths Dash have created a high-frequency replenishment channel with better margin visibility and lower trade investment than traditional retail.

Wholesale: Reach vs Margin

Lowest margin channel (wholesale pricing) but highest geographic reach into informal trade. Key entry point for Limpopo, Mpumalanga, and Northern Cape markets where modern retail penetration is below 40%.

Dominant in the mixer segment with 78% category share. On-trade is the primary channel — bars, restaurants, and hotels account for 62% of Schweppes volume. Premium positioning protects margin.

Smallest brand by SKU count but high per-unit margin. Currently under-distributed (Wt. Dist. 44%) relative to the sports nutrition trend. Gym channel and Petrol & Travel are priority expansion targets.

Key Findings

Strategic Insights

From 8,000 CCPB sales transactions, 246 SKUs, and 6 trade channels across 9 provinces.

Gauteng Concentration Risk

Gauteng generates 32% of total revenue — disproportionate vs. 26% population share. Revenue per capita is R2,580 vs. national average of R1,210. This concentration creates vulnerability; a Gauteng economic shock would disproportionately impact overall performance.

Energy Drinks: Fastest Growing

Monster and Powerplay combined represent 6% revenue but 22% YoY growth — the highest category growth rate in the portfolio. At current trajectory, energy drinks will be the #2 category by volume within 3 years, overtaking Sprite and Fanta.

28% of SKUs are D-Grade

10% of SKUs classified D-grade represent dead weight in the range — they consume trade spend, warehouse space, and sales force attention while contributing negative margin contribution after overheads. SKU rationalisation targeting D-grades could improve portfolio margin by 2-3 percentage points.

Convenience: Best Margin Channel

Despite only 20% revenue share, Convenience delivers the highest gross margin per rand sold — with minimal trade investment (3-5%) relative to mass retail (12-15%). Redirecting 5% of mass retail trade spend to convenience activation would generate equivalent volume at 40% higher margin.

CCPB Model Highlights

Price-by-Channel
Each SKU carries up to 6 different price points — one per channel. The product master tracks list_price, std_cost, and margin at the EA and CS (case) UOM level enabling true channel-level profitability.
Weighted Distribution
Weighted distribution (Wt. Dist.) is the share of category volume sold through outlets that stock the SKU. It's the primary measure of availability — a SKU with 100% Wt. Dist. is available where all the volume is being bought.
Trade Spend ROI
Trade spend (promotional investment to retailers) averages 8% of revenue. The ROI model targets above 3.5x net revenue return per rand spent — below this hurdle, trade investment destroys value relative to base business margin.
